How we work

AI in the workflow.

Claude and other AI tools sit inside the ordinary loop of a small studio: drafting, building, and checking the work. They shorten some steps. They do not replace the people making the games and the tools.

01

Draft

Notes, interface copy, and internal writing often begin with Claude or another model. We rewrite until the words belong to the studio.

02

Build

Game code, refactors, and the developer tools around a project are written with AI assistance. What we keep is read in the codebase, not accepted from a chat window.

03

Decide

A model can point at a rough edge. What is fun, what is finished, and what is ready to show someone else is still a human call.

We describe the practice here. We do not publish performance figures.
